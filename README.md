Entry for the [make.fun bounty "The Robot Pickup Line Championship"](https://make.fun/bounty/u-jolly-fox-32-the-robot-pickup-line-championship-3c177b1b): one pickup line, a single line of at most 200 characters, in the voice of a robot trying to charm someone.

`main.py` writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`:

> My warranty covers everything except this: I was built to last fifty years, and I already want to spend all of them standing next to you.

The joke grows out of being a machine — a warranty, a designed lifespan of fifty years — and the twist lands on the last words: the robot already wants to spend all of them standing next to you.
